I'm an M.S. Computer Science student at **UMass Amherst** and a Graduate Research Assistant at **Argonne National Laboratory**. I have 2+ years of research and engineering experience across ML systems, distributed infrastructure, federated learning, and LLM post-training. I'm looking for full-time opportunities starting after graduation.

At Argonne, I work on **large-scale LLM post-training** and **federated learning**, building distributed training systems and tooling that support ML research across scientific domains.

I built and open-sourced [HiveWatch](https://github.com/APPFL/hivewatch/), a distributed-training observability toolkit adopted within [APPFL](https://github.com/APPFL/APPFL), and built Kubernetes-based researcher environments on NERSC Spin. Previously, I helped scale [Flotilla](https://github.com/dream-lab/flotilla/) at IISc and co-developed [FedProj](https://openreview.net/forum?id=lhTWPh3Tjm), published in TMLR. My work also spans confidential computing and secure federated learning.

I'm particularly interested in **GPU programming, LLM serving, and scientific ML**. Our work on *[Towards Global Federated Genome-Wide Association Meta-Analysis Using GA4GH TES](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02227)* received a **Best Poster Award at IEEE eScience**.
